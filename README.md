## Bill Weakley

Senior iOS engineer, 25 years in. Principal consultant at Bill Weakley LLC, working remote from Kennebunk, Maine.

Most of my career is native mobile: shipping Swift and Objective-C at enterprise
scale — a payments suite with 400,000+ installs, an Objective-C to Swift
migration onto MVVM and dependency injection at zero downtime, accessibility
work done alongside Apple's own engineers, and a BLE wearable with paired iOS and
Android apps.

For the last two years I have been doing something adjacent to that: going into
existing client codebases and setting them up so agentic AI code generation
actually works there — custom agents and skills, Model Context Protocol
integrations, and spec-driven workflows — so a team can keep using them after I
leave. The 25 years is the qualification for that work, not a footnote to it.
Knowing where a large codebase hides its landmines is most of the job.
